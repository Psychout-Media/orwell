
## Amendment — the player is plugged in: the narrator never speaks, acts or decides for them, and never hands their words to a houseguest (ORW-725, 2026-11-19)

A season-2 playtest showed two recurring model failures: the narrator wrote the player in the first person (an action, then a quoted answer the player never typed, or a decision they never made), and a houseguest recited the player's own message back as that houseguest's speech. The rule is not new; it was only stated for one case (a direct question to the player is a hard stop). It now rides every live house turn.

- **Prompt (rule only, per-turn):** the terminal HARD-CONSTRAINTS block carries one `PLAYER'S VOICE` line (`renderHardConstraints`): the player's words, actions and decisions are only what their own message of this turn states, spoken of in the second person; no line, action, choice or answer is written for them; no houseguest is given the player's words as their speech. It rides the per-turn block, not `BASE_GAME_MASTER_PROMPT` (no budget change).
- **Net (front-end, narrow, fail-open):** `frontend/src/player_voice_guard.py`, called from `agent_loop._player_voice_guard` at both emit sites beside the other pre-emission walls. It withholds a whole sentence (never rewrites, never authors) in a started game when it is (1) an unquoted first-person physical-action sentence and the unattributed quote after it, (2) `You say, "..."` where the quote is not in the player's own message, (3) `you decide/agree/accept/choose/vote/promise/resolve` that the player's message did not state (hedged and question forms are skipped), or (4) a houseguest question that repeats a long run of a question the player typed (statements, deal terms and an attributed "you said" are never judged). Off with `ORWELL_PLAYER_VOICE_GUARD=0`; belt `player-voice-withheld`, counted only when a sentence was withheld. The quiet rewrite (`ORWELL_PREDISPLAY_REWRITE`) is untouched and stays off.
- **What the net cannot do:** it is a detector for these shapes. A model that voices the player in a shape outside them (a paraphrase, a feeling, an action in the second person that is not a decision verb) is not caught, and a houseguest echoing a short phrase of the player's (under the run lengths above) is deliberately left alone as ordinary conversation.
- **Wall (unchanged, now pinned):** `tests/unit/playerVoiceWall.test.ts` plants a sentinel in the player's words for one witness and proves no houseguest outside the scene holds it.
