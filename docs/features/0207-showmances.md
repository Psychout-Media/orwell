# 0207 - Showmances and attraction (Theme A, ORW-657..661)

Cards: ORW-657 (theme), ORW-658 A1, ORW-659 A2, ORW-660 A3, ORW-661 A4. Ryne approved all four slices 2026-11-18. Executable spec: `0207-showmances.feature` (spec-only until the build). Foundation: `src/engine/attraction.ts` + `tests/unit/attraction.test.ts` (built, BDD Auditor). The slices: Overseers A and B, through the Coordinator.

## What exists today (build on it, don't duplicate it)
- **Seeded showmances** (`seededRels.showmances`, 0059): engine-picked couples with stages; `showmancePartnerOf` feeds house life's flirt/cuddle (`houseLife.ts`, `v.showmance`).
- **The orientation rule** `diversity.ts#showmancePlausible` (both sides). `canBeDrawnTo` is the same rule read one way; keep them in step.
- **Romance scenes** fold as `showmance` (`bondRecord`: `romance` count, `warmth`; kind `showmance` at `BOND.showmanceScenes`). Faked distance (0184 2b) already hides romance scenes from onlookers.

## The model (foundation)
- **Attraction is one-sided and private.** A can be drawn to B whatever B feels, only within A's own orientation (`canBeDrawnTo`).
- **Chemistry** is a ceiling per ORDERED pair from its own keyed roll (`chemistryOf`): most pairs none (`ATTRACTION.chemistryShare` 0.2 of eligible pairs have any).
- **It grows with time together** (`attractionNow`, `growthPerDayTogether` per shared day) and never past the ceiling.
- **It shows only in behaviour:** who someone seeks out (`seekPull`), sits near, protects. Never a number or a label, never a list of lines; the narrator voices it.

## Slices and seams
- **A1 (ORW-658) attraction in house life.**
  - Store: `live.chemistry?: Record<chemistryKey, number>`, drawn once at season start on a keyed side stream (one roll per ordered pair; headless byte-identical: played games only), plus days-together counted from shared house-life scenes.
  - House life: add `seekPull(attractionNow(...))` to the partner score when choosing whom to spend time with; flirt/cuddle can start from a mutual-enough attraction, not only a seeded showmance. Seeded showmances stay (they start with chemistry already high).
  - The player as object: an NPC can be drawn to the player only once the player has stated their gender (casting or later); undefined means never. The player's own attraction is never modelled.
- **A2 (ORW-659) the couple is a target.**
  - `readsAsCouple(perceivedBond, romanceSeenOrTold)` per reader: own-view perceived bond (ORW-574) plus a romance scene the reader registered or a told claim.
  - Nominations: an HOH outside a pair it reads as a couple adds `coupleNomPush` to one of them. Measure with the stacking report (a new decision term).
- **A3 (ORW-660) heart against game.**
  - At a vote where the partner and an alliance pull apart: `heartWins(romanceTie, allianceTie, mutual)`; the loser tie takes a strain either way (shaken).
  - Breakup: `BREAKUP` deltas (deeper than `BETRAYAL_SHOCK`), a grudge-book entry (ORW-607), the showmance stage to resolved.
- **A4 (ORW-661) the player's showmance.**
  - `playerShowmance(scenesThePlayerStarted, npcDrawnToPlayer)`: only by the player's own acts, answered by an NPC drawn to them. The engine never decides it.
  - Then the house notices (as A2), the partner expects loyalty (a broken expectation is a betrayal, ORW-646's claims path), rivals use it. An NPC the player turns down reacts in character (narrated, from their profile).
- **Flag:** `ORWELL_SHOWMANCES`, default ON, sorted into the flag files.
- **Measure:** the 12-season mix (romance share will rise; say by how much), the stacking report for A2/A3, jury reach and gradient unchanged (played only).

## The rules
- The player in both directions: NPCs can be drawn to the player and the player can start one; never assume the player's gender or orientation.
- No short lists: no player-facing words from a list; the narrator voices attraction from behaviour.
- The wall: attraction and chemistry are engine-only; a couple is read only from what the reader saw or was told.
- Never shown as numbers.

## Amendment 2026-11-18 (ORW-636 follow-up): the couple term never fired; why, and the fix
The stacking report found `nomination-*.couple` and `replacement.couple` never non-zero in played chat-paced seasons. A funnel over 8 seeds x 1500 messages (`tests/calibration/coupleFunnel.test.ts`, report only, `ORWELL_COUPLE_FUNNEL=1`) showed every upstream stage working: 17-30 ordered pairs per season with attraction at or above the flirt bar, 2-68 romance scenes (flirt/cuddle) in 1-3 pairs, most of them seen by someone, and 6-23 reader/pair combinations per season that had caught a romance. It dropped to zero at the last stage: **no reader's own-view perceived bond of a pair ever reached 0.65.** Cause: a houseguest's read of two OTHERS is the edge baseline (0.25) plus `floaterBondSpan` (0.35) times what they saw, so it tops out at 0.60; the foundation's `coupleBondAtLeast` (0.65, on the whole 0..1 bond scale) sat above the ceiling of the read it was judged against. The existing unit tests stubbed `perceivedBond` to 0.9, which hid it.
Fix (wiring/scale, no new flag, flag off byte-identical): in a played game with the own-view read on, `couplePushFor` judges the pair on the read's own scale, `readsAsCoupleOwnView` (`attractionLife.ts`): the share of the way from the baseline to the ceiling must be at least `ATTRACTION_LIFE.coupleReadShare` (0.7, three net warm scenes watched, plus a romance seen or told). The blended unplayed read keeps the foundation's bar. `coupleNomPush` (0.25) is untouched. Proof: a unit test with a real (unstubbed) read, red on main.

## Amendment 2026-11-20 (ORW-736, PO ruling from Ryne): a showmance is a slow build over weeks, not a switch
Ruling: "showmances are something like a slower build over several weeks. Not like a switch turned on." Flag `ORWELL_SHOWMANCE_PACING`, default ON (`=0` = the old affinity-threshold stages, byte-identical); a played game with `ORWELL_SHOWMANCES` only; headless sims never reach it.

**Measured on main first** (`tests/calibration/showmanceTimingReport.test.ts`, `ORWELL_SHOWMANCE_TIMING_REPORT=1`, 8 seeds x 1500 messages, an active player who keeps a romance going, the player's presentation a seeded pick). The old machine was `nextShowmanceStage` over mutual affinity alone, run once per player turn on the seeded pairs only, with no days in it: 7 seeded pairs; first `bond` on in-game day 2 / 3 (median) / 36 (max); first `visible` (the public couple, the moment the narrator may name them) on day 1 / 2 (median) / 5 (max), so 6 of 7 pairs were public in week 1 and the narrator's own list was non-empty during week 1; bond to visible took a median of 1 day. A pair with the player had no stage at all (the player's romance only ever showed as a `live.romances` entry, day 2 median). On a 3-seed x 3000-message run: both seeded pairs public on day 2 and day 3. This is the "visible by Week 2 with nothing seen on screen" Fable found.

**The pace machine** (`src/engine/showmancePace.ts`, pure; constants `SHOWMANCE_PACE` in `attractionLife.ts`, each with its reason). One machine for every pair: the seeded NPC pairs, every other pair the bond record has read as a showmance (engine-only record), and the player's own romance. Levels: 0 spark, 1 bond, 2 sensed (a close ally of either has caught it), 3 noticed (the room has), 4 known (house-wide: the public couple). Stage mapping for every existing reader: 0 spark, 1-3 bond, 4 visible.
- A step up needs ALL of: the least days in the level before it (3, 4, 5, 6 days, scaled by the pair's own keyed pace factor 1 +/- 0.25 on `${seed}:showmance-pace:${pairKey}`), chemistry accrued past that level's bar (1.5, 3, 4.5, 6.5 chemistry-days: shared days x the pair's mutual chemistry; a pair shares about a quarter of a day per day), and, from level 2, people who caught it (sensed: one close ally; noticed: 30 percent of the others; known: 60 percent), seen or told. The public couple also needs the in-game day to be at least 22 (about week 5), derived from days not a week number.
- STALL: no progress while the pair spend no time together (a day's grace, then it cools by 0.25 chemistry-days a day) and none while either is on the block (it neither builds nor cools). FADE: a level whose progress falls below 70 percent of its own bar falls back one step, recorded; a couple the whole house knows does not become unknown (it can only end: A3 breakup, an eviction).
- NOTICING in steps: each step is appended to `live.showmancePace[pairKey].steps` with the houseguests who had caught the romance at that moment (the player included only if the player was among them). Append-only, engine-only, never projected (superset guard in `sessionSnapshot`). Only the last step (known) records the house beat the player can see (`onShowmanceSurfaced`), exactly as before.
- The PLAYER'S romance runs the same machine, same minimums, same bars. The engine never advances it for the player: the pair exists only from the player's own acts (A4), it moves only with the shared time the player actually spends with them (the player's own side is never modelled, so the houseguest's chemistry is the one read), and nobody is told it is happening.
- Composition with the narrator gate (ORW-733 follow-up, not built here): the record holds who had caught each step, so "the stage the house knows" and "what the player saw or was told" are separate reads; the narrator list (`visibleShowmances`) still reads the seeded `visible` stage and is not widened.

**After** (flag on, same seeds and driver): first `bond` day 7 / 10 (median) / 12 (max); first `sensed` day 15 / 15.5 / 23 (4 pairs); first `noticed` day 20 / 23.5 / 27 (2 pairs); no public couple in the 8 seasons of 1500 messages (a season that length is only about 36 days); the narrator's list was empty in week 1. On the 3000-message runs (57-61 days) a seeded pair became a public couple on day 38 (week 8): bond d11, sensed d20, noticed d29, known d37; the other pair reached sensed (d9, d18). The player's own pair climbs under the same minimums when the player keeps the time (BDD: "The player's own romance builds at the same pace"). The couple term (A2) is untouched: it reads the reader's own-view bond plus a romance scene seen or told, not the stage; the funnel (`ORWELL_COUPLE_FUNNEL=1`, 8 seeds x 1500) still fires it in played seasons (HOH steps with a couple push: 102, 1129, 781, 0, 919, 654, 0, 198 of ~1150 per season). Calibration: 12-season mix guard 29.0/34.8/26.9/9.2 (main alone about 29.1/34.6/26.7/9.6; reference 30.2/34.7/28.5/6.7), jury reach gate passes.

**Executable now (ORW-742):** the six original scenarios plus the pacing scenarios are real BDD (`features/step_definitions/showmances_0207.steps.ts`): each plays real seasons through the house-mix driver or drives the real writers; none sets chemistry, stages, bonds or days by hand.

**Not built (each needs a card):** (1) heart against game is checked in BDD at the pure rule only; the live fold still has its coverage in `tests/unit/showmanceHeartBreakup.test.ts`, which sets bonds by hand, so a real-path live test (a vote with an alliance pulling against a player's partner) is open. (2) An NPC pair that the bond record reads as a romance but that was not seeded gets a pace record but no narrator or house beat at "known" (the narrator list is still seeded pairs only); a public couple for an emergent pair is open. (3) The narrator gate on the player's seenBy layer (ORW-733 follow-up) is open. (4) A "told" path for noticing (gossip carrying a couple to someone who did not see it) is not wired: only witnessed scenes count toward the noticing steps. (5) The seeded-pair budget means about half the seasons have no seeded showmance; there is no minimum.

## Amendment 2026-11-23 (ORW-767, Vault Wall): a showmance reaches the player by being TOLD
Ryne's rule: what the player knows about someone is only what they saw or were told. The narrator's `visibleShowmances()` was gated on `romanceSeenBy(player, a, b)` (a scene the player was in or watched) alone; a pair a houseguest tells the player about had no route (`ClaimKind` had no couple kind, a knowledge fact has one subject). Flag `ORWELL_COUPLE_TOLD`, default ON (`=0` = byte-identical; played games only). Engine in `src/engine/coupleTold.ts`; wiring in `GameSessionAdapter` (`noteCoupleSeen`, `noteCoupleTalk`, `tellCoupleToPlayer`, `playerToldCouple`) and one composition seam (`setOnCoupleTold`, `registry.ts`).
- **The claim.** One additive member of the closed `ClaimKind` union in `src/engine/beliefStore.ts` (Coordinator-authorised, Auditor-owned file): `"couple"`, subject and object the pair ordered by id. Nothing else in that file moved; every reader switches or filters on named kinds, so the new kind is inert to all of them. On the player's side the pair is a knowledge fact with the lineage id `couple:<a>|<b>` (the fact still has one subject, the pair rides on the lineage id) through a `told-by:<teller>` pathway, with the teller's confidence.
- **Writers (existing belief layer, keyed side streams `${seed}:couple-told:*`, never the shared stream).** (1) A houseguest who saw a romance scene (in the room, or noticed from it: the scene's `seenBy`) holds the pair first-hand. (2) In a speech scene a houseguest may pass ONE couple they hold to the other person (`COUPLE_TOLD.talkChance`), second-hand (confidence times `retoldFactor`), and now and then the wrong partner is heard (`mishearChance`): a misheard pair is a belief that was never true. (3) A houseguest with the player, when the existing claims-to-player decision tells nothing else, may tell the player one through the same trust and crowd gates (`tellChance`), voiced as `tellsYou` kind `couple-with` (the teller's own words, a certainty band; never the truth).
- **The reads.** `romanceSeenBy(player, a, b)` now also holds when the player was TOLD that pair: it feeds the narrator gate and the 0207 pace record's player-as-observer (the closed "told path for noticing" open item above, for the player). It does NOT change what an NPC reader sees (`couplePushFor` is untouched), so no nomination moves.
- **The wall.** A told pair is the player's belief with a source and a confidence. The narrator's showmance LIST still needs a real pair at the stage the house knows AND the player having seen or been told it: a misheard (false) pair is told and held but never listed, so a belief is never upgraded to the truth. Flag off: nothing is written, nothing is read.
- **Gates.** `tests/unit/orw767CoupleTold.test.ts` (played through the house-mix driver; roles only; Vault-free surface check; flag-off control; save/load round trip).

## Amendment 2026-11-25 (ORW-892): attraction shows as flirts and cuddles outside the seeded showmances

Played games only. A person drawn to someone who is in the room with them (attraction at least `ATTRACTION_LIFE.flirtAtLeast`, the other flirtable, never the player) gets a flirt or a cuddle with chance `HOUSE_LIFE.romanceChance` x culture x min(1, attraction / `ATTRACTION_LIFE.flirtFullAt`) before the seek roll; the seek now only sends them toward someone who is not there (a check-in). Before this, outside the seeded pairs a played season had no flirt at all (the attraction-seek yielded check-ins only). `flirtFullAt` is 2 (ORW-702 slice 2): attraction is at most 1, so the chance never reaches the full romance chance. Measured: with flirts off a played season reached a public couple in about 1 of 4 seasons; with them on, about half (6 of 12) at 2 and 3 of 4 at 0.3; the chance is not what limits public couples (the pace machine of ORW-736 is), so the 'about 1 in 3' target is NOT met by this lever alone (ORW-892 follow-up).
