# 0172 - Groups: roles, nesting, spies and cracking (Theme 3 slice 6), with ORW-603 and ORW-611

Executable spec: `0172-groups-roles-and-cracking.feature` (wired into `cucumber.cjs`). Flag `ORWELL_GROUP_ROLES` (default ON, `=0` is the kill switch; played games only).

## What is built
- **ORW-603 (a bug):** a pitched alliance's roster is frozen at what the player was told (`Alliance.toldRoster`, set by `reveal(id, PLAYER, told)`); the player's pitch view shows it, never the live membership. The history keeps the live roster.
- **Roles** (engine keys: leader, strategist, shield, swing, glue, comp-threat), dealt once a day from the members' own bonds (`bondReader().tie`), the house graph (hub, broker) and the character's stats; a keyed roll, then the id, breaks a tie. Every member holds one; a member who leaves drops theirs; a newcomer is dealt one.
- **Nesting:** a triple of an alliance of four or more who stand closer to each other than to the rest is an inner circle (a record on the group, not a second alliance in the list).
- **Purpose** (survival, power, endgame) from how much of the starting cast is left; each shift is a history entry.
- **Private cut orders and the agreed target:** each member keeps their own first three names (threat to my game, comp, my tie); in survival the group's own members are off the order. The majority's target is the Borda head of the outsiders; settling on one moves each member's own threat read of them by one bounded step.
- **Spy:** an awake outsider with no alliance, close to the founder and to someone outside, is drawn on a keyed daily chance; they join, and when the group settles a target they tell the person they work for in a hidden two-person scene (`spy-report`). If the target is that person, their read of the planners turns. The player witnesses it only if they were in the room.
- **Shadow group:** the group holding the crown, with someone it means to cut who is warm with other outsiders, draws a counter-alliance of those outsiders; it is a normal alliance the first group is not part of, tagged `shadowOf`.
- **Cracking:** once the purpose has moved past survival, members whose own first cut is a fellow member split from the rest on a keyed daily chance; two or more found a new alliance, one alone leaves. The history keeps a `cracked` entry with the whole roster, a `left` per leaver and the new `formed`; nothing is deleted.
- **ORW-611, the player plugged in:** (1) a hostile plan aimed at the player (get-them-out or revenge) is an approach raised through the desire clock's own path, with no trust floor (a hostile houseguest is not pressing a request); (2) a pull-them-in goal whose target is the player pitches the player the holder's alliance through the existing pitch path; (3) a group that settles on the player sends one member to press them (the same approach path), and withdraws a pitch the player has not taken when the founder no longer wants them in or the group has settled on them (an event the player is in, naming the alliance, never a roster).

## Follow-up (owner rulings 2026-11-17) (ORW-825)
- **A spy can turn loyal.** Once planted, a spy's own warmth for the group (the mean directed bond to its members, `BondReader.strength`) is weighed daily against the bond to the one they work for. Only when the first outweighs the second does a keyed roll (`spy-turn`) turn them, the chance growing with the margin (never above `turnMax`). A turned spy confesses (share set by their candor), double-crosses (keeps reporting but names a wrong target, share set by cunning among the less candid) or just stops. History kinds `spy-turned` (detail: quiet, confess, double-cross) and `spy-confessed`; history only, skipped by the event flush.
- **The shadow trigger is broader.** A group dominates when it holds the HOH, or is more than half of the houseguests left, or carried the last two evictions (the evictee was its agreed target). The counter-alliance is formed by outsiders whose OWN mean threat read of the group's members is at least `shadowThreat`, most threatened first.
- **The player's alliances live like any other (ORW-616, all four equal).** With group life on, the player's alliances fade (same cohesion rule and days as an NPC one), break over a member's hot goal against a fellow member (the player can be the target), and go through roles, cut orders, the agreed target, spies, shadows and cracking among their NPC members. The player is never dealt a role. The player is on a cut order only when an NPC member's OWN active get-them-out or revenge goal names them (then first on that member's order); a group past survival whose members aim their first cut at the player breaks away. The player learns only by an observable act: a member's turning on the alliance (the betrayal event), the break-away event (names the alliance and ONE leaver, never a roster), the fade event (names the alliance, no roster). The told roster of a pitch is untouched (ORW-603). No quiet deal break is introduced, so `DealLedger.learnOfBreak` is not used. Nominations and votes change only through the members' own threat reads (the bounded steps); the cut order and the agreed target move no nomination code.
- **Review notes.** The cut-order threat step keeps a `bumped` list per alliance and is taken once per target, however often the agreed target flips back. A spy's report is overheard only when the spy AND the one they report to are in the player's room and the player is awake.
- **Told claims.** A spy's report is ONE claim (`tellClaim`) about the member who drives the plan (the leader when their own first cut is the target, else the first member whose first cut it is): `targets-you` when the listener is the target, else `going-after`; true only when it names the agreed target; `mislead` when the spy lies. A member who breaks away tells the target (if warm enough) the same way. The PLAYER is told only when the speaker really talks to them (same room and both awake, or on the desire clock's approach): a spy who has turned (when the group means the player), or a breakaway. The learned-of-the-break pathway is the Auditor's.

## Left out on purpose
- The agreed target moves nominations and votes only through each member's own threat read (a bounded one-time step); no nomination code was touched.

## Vault Wall
`live.groupLife` (roles, inner circle, cut orders, spy, shadow link) and the group-life history entries are Vault-class: no view, tool result, narrator prompt or admin projection carries them (the BDD scenario checks the live surfaces). New history kinds are history-only (`GROUP_CHANGES`); the event flush skips them.

## Streams
No shared stream is drawn: every roll is a keyed `agendaRoll` (or a name draw on its own `SeededRandom`), so a flag-off season is byte-identical.

## Amendment 2026-11-18 (ORW-611, the player plugged in: a step aimed at the player is recorded)

Premise check against main: the card's second and third claims were already built by slice 6 (a hostile goal aimed at the player is raised as an open approach without the trust floor, a pull-them-in goal whose target is the player is a pitch through the player's own consent, a group that settles on the player presses them and withdraws a pitch it no longer wants). The first claim was still true: a goal step aimed at the player was raised as an approach but recorded nothing. Now, behind `ORWELL_PLAYER_AIMED` (default ON, played games only, `=0` is byte-identical): the step is written to an append-only `aimedAtPlayer` record (who, which goal kind, hostile or not, status); when it surfaces as the approach the player sees, the daily tick records a player-witnessed event (never hidden); talking to that houseguest or striking a deal with them answers it; a hostile one nobody answers within the answer window closes as `ignored` and costs that houseguest's read of the player (the existing squeeze shape, bounded, on its own keyed stream); a non-hostile one fades. The engine never answers for the player, and nothing is removed.
