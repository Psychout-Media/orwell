## 2. Top findings, ranked by payoff

### #1 — Eviction-night (and finale) sub-beats tick the in-game clock 3h each, so every eviction night wraps a full day — LIKELY (traced end to end, not executed)
- **Evidence:**
  - `INERT_BEATS` = `comp-elimination`, `day-break`, `exit-interview` only (`src/engine/liveSeason.ts:145`).
  - `isInertBeat` is consulted in exactly one place: the per-beat clock gate in `advanceOneBeat` (`GameSessionAdapter.ts:8633`). Every non-inert BeatEvent calls `advanceClock`.
  - `eviction-reveal`, `eviction-goodbye` and `eviction-result` are absent from `DURATION_CATEGORY` (`daySchedule.ts:108-116`), so they take the flat `CLOCK.perBeatHours = 3` (`sleepConstants.ts:67`).
  - One eviction night emits about 5 batched reveals (`STAGED_EVICTION_TARGET_ROUNDS = 5`, `liveSeason.ts:964`), the `eviction` beat (2h), 3 NPC goodbyes (`GOODBYE_MESSAGE_COUNT = 3`, `:1971`) and the result. That is ~26–29h added to a day that starts at 8 (`day-break` before eviction, `:2493-2500`). The day ends at hour 32 (`DAY_END_HOUR`), so the clock always wraps.
  - The wrap path (`advanceClockBy` wrapAtEnd, `liveSeason.ts:1683-1692`) sets `lastSleepDepth = DAY_END_HOUR` ("up to the bitter end ⇒ the maximum sleep debt"). `advanceOneBeat` then runs `accrueNightFatigue()` (`GameSessionAdapter.ts:8648`).
  - Nothing re-anchors the clock before the next HOH: `rollWeek` re-arms only the noms/veto/ceremony/eviction day-breaks (`liveSeason.ts:1836-1840`). The next HOH comp lands around evening or night with max rest debt.
  - The code's own comment calls these "a ~14-advance slog of inert beats … PRESENTATION ONLY" (`:2073-2077`), so the omission looks unintended. #537 fixed exactly this shape for staged comp drops, and 0130 correctly added `exit-interview`.
  - The finale has the same issue: about 20 `finale` beats (2h each, `DURATION_CATEGORY.finale`) plus 9 `finale-reveal` (3h) (`liveSeason.ts:2314-2365`).
  - No test pins the clock across an eviction night. `nightGateNeutral`/`perConversationClockNeutral` only prove clock-off byte-identity.
- **What a player would notice:** the HUD flips through a whole night during the vote reading and goodbyes. Houseguests are "asleep" during their own goodbyes. The next HOH comp happens at night, and the player silently carries the maximum hidden sleep penalty into it (`ORWELL_SOCIAL_FATIGUE` is default-ON, so social folds also skew negative). The player never chose to stay up, which violates ADR 0006's "player owns bedtime" principle.
- **Fix size:** S. Make the eviction/finale presentation sub-beats clock-silent: add them to `INERT_BEATS` (its only consumer is the clock gate), or create a separate clock-silent set. Optionally re-anchor the next HOH to the morning after.
- **Dependency:** this changes the recorded time-of-day stream, so it **stales the golden fixture** and needs a re-record in the same PR. It is calibration-neutral: the clock is off in the sims.

### #2 — `exit-interview` is missing from the model-facing `submitDecision` schema enum — VERIFIED (schema) / LIKELY (impact)
- **Evidence:**
  - The enum at `frontend/src/tool_schemas.py:1384-1389` lists every kind except `exit-interview` (and `secret-veto`).
  - The field descriptions (`:1397-1398`) document `vote`/`statement` for goodbye-message but say nothing about exit-interview riding `vote`.
  - The relay (`tool_implementations.py:5245-5254`) and both route allowlists (`orwell_routes.py:1562-1571`, ws per `test_sync_audit.py:105-107`) do accept it.
  - The pending barrier tells the model to "submit it via submitDecision" (`chat_helpers.py` `_PENDING_GENERAL_HINT`). That conflicts with the schema.
  - No test checks schema-enum ↔ `_DECISION_KINDS` parity. `test_sync_audit.py` covers only the routes.
- **What a player would notice:** when the player is evicted and types their exit-interview answer in chat, the narrator cannot submit it. The game sits at the pending until the player finds and uses the card.
- **Fix size:** S. Add the kind to the enum, describe stance-via-`vote`, and add a parity test.
- **Related, outside this band:** `secret-veto` (0025) is missing from the enum, the relay, both route allowlists, the card and both fast-forward resolvers. It is latent only because `ORWELL_REACTIVE_TWISTS` defaults off and is not in deploy defaults (`GameSessionAdapter.ts:1635`). Turning it on would freeze a player who holds the safety: the holder is the lowest-résumé houseguest (`liveSeason.ts:1895-1897`).

### #3 — The 0048 retrospective panel drops the 0130 exit-interview reel, the #1396 pull-quote reel and the player's own confessionals — VERIFIED
- **Evidence:**
  - The engine returns `exitInterviews`, `pullQuoteReel` and `playerConfessionals` (`GameSessionAdapter.ts:3150-3172`).
  - The route passes the whole object through (`orwell_routes.py:959-980`).
  - `frontend/static/js/orwellRetrospective.js` renders only `twists`, `evictionVotes`, `juryVotes` and `hiddenStory` (`:197-260`). A grep for `exitInterviews|pullQuoteReel` across `frontend/static/js`, `frontend/src` and `frontend/routes` finds nothing.
  - The spec claims exit interviews are "carried into the 0048 season-end retrospective" (`0130.md:7`). They reach the player only if the narrator happens to call `seasonRetrospective` and voice them.
- **What a player would notice:** the season-end "untold story" never shows the exit-interview montage, the weekly confessional pull-quotes, or the player's own Diary Room through-line. Built content is invisible at the game's biggest payoff screen.
- **Fix size:** S–M (three render blocks in one file). The PO asked to playtest 0048 before refining, so these could be folded into that pass.

### #4 — The F14 post-goodbye auto-advance now lands on the exit-interview beat, not the eviction result — LIKELY
- **Evidence:**
  - `orwell_routes.py:258-271` fires ONE `advance_game` after the player's goodbye. Its rationale: "the engine still owes goodbye → eviction-result → rollWeek".
  - Since 0130, the stage after goodbyes is `exit-interview` (`liveSeason.ts:2115-2135`). For an NPC evictee that one advance yields only the exit-interview beat. The result and `rollWeek` need a further advance, from the stall-nudge or forced-advance belts.
  - `AUTO_ADVANCE_PENDING_KINDS` (`GameSessionAdapter.ts:793-796`) also excludes `goodbye-message` and `exit-interview`.
- **What a player would notice:** after writing their goodbye, the house can hang on the exit interview for a turn before the week rolls. The belts should recover it, but it is an extra lull at a high-drama moment.
- **Fix size:** S. Loop the F14 tail until the eviction stage clears, or add the kinds to the auto-advance set.

### #5 — Both fast-forward resolvers send the wrong field for `exit-interview`, so a player-evicted fast-forward cannot finish — VERIFIED
- **Evidence:**
  - `frontend/scripts/responsive_matrix.py:228-233` and `docs/audits/playtest-harness/s4ff.mjs:47` send `{stance, message}`.
  - The engine accepts only `vote`/`choice` (`decisionFields.ts:14-18` `singlePickId`; `GameSessionAdapter.ts:10269-10274`), so it throws "a legal exit stance is required".
  - In `finish_game` that becomes a non-200 → `None`. After more than 5 consecutive failures the loop breaks unfinished (`responsive_matrix.py:266-291`). s4ff hard-loops similarly.
  - The comment in responsive_matrix claims this case was the fix for the freeze ("pick the first legal posture … so the finish loop clears the eviction"), but the payload shape is wrong. The engine's own internal mapping uses `vote: input.stance` (`GameSessionAdapter.ts:3240`), and the pending view already carries `options` (`:10383-10389`), so the generic `vote: opt(0)` would have worked.
- **What the owner would notice:** responsive-matrix "finish season" runs and s4ff playtests fail to reach the endgame or retro whenever the seeded player is evicted. Endgame-card and retro audit coverage silently drop out.
- **Fix size:** S. Send `{kind, vote: stance, statement}`.

### #6 — 0046: a pre-jury evictee does not get the specced "terminal season-end state" — LIKELY
- **Evidence:** `finished` is set only at the finale or a self-evict (`liveSeason.ts:1963,2363`). The retrospective requires `finished` (`GameSessionAdapter.ts:3002`). Spec `0046.md:6,38-40` promises a terminal season-end for a pre-jury eviction. The player status reads `evicted` (`GameSessionAdapter.ts:11132-11147`), but the season keeps running.
- **What a player would notice:** after a week-2 eviction the player must keep advancing about 10 weeks of NPC play before the Vault unseals. "Season over" is not actually over.
- **Fix size:** M. Auto-fast-forward to finished on a pre-jury player eviction (reuse the `advanceToFinale` L38 driver), or amend the spec. Needs a PO call.
