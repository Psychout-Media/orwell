## Findings (BDD Auditor, 2026-11-13)
- **Advance-only play:** about 17 off-screen scenes a game day across the house, about 2.3 per houseguest, and on **half of all houseguest-days (52%) a houseguest has no off-screen scene at all**. Always the same 7 types, in nearly the same shares across seeds.
- **Chat-paced play: about 109 scenes a game day (about 14.6 per houseguest), roughly 6 times the advance-only rate.** The orchestrator is meant to debounce the society tick to one per `SOCIETY_TICK_HOURS` (1.5 game hours) of social play, which would give about 30 to 35 scenes on a 16-hour waking day; 109 means ticks run far more often than that while the player chats. Cause NOT diagnosed here (a candidate: a chat commit that changes some non-clock live field counts as "progressed" and takes the full tick path instead of the debounced aux path, orchestrator.ts ~500-525). Flagged for ORW-315, which replaces this tick, to check against its own one-action-per-awake-houseguest-per-hour budget.
- **Persistence of intent** reads high under chat play (91% seek the same partner the next day) mostly because the pair draw favors the strongest ties and runs so often, not because any plan carries over (no goal is stored today).
- **Fit to the initiator's drive** is about 58% in both modes: the type is drawn from a weight table after the pair is chosen, so the drive shapes the scene only loosely.
- Other hidden events alongside the scenes: deals, confessionals and generic `evt` records (the chat-paced driver produces many more confessionals and `evt` records per day, consistent with the extra ticks).
