## Findings (BDD Auditor, 2026-11-13)
- **Advance-only (headless) is identical to the baseline**, as designed: house life runs only in a played game, so the calibration spine is untouched.
- **Chat-paced play now has about 53 off-screen scenes a game day, about 7.1 per houseguest** (old stretch after ORW-323: about 18). That is the design working: every awake houseguest acts each society tick instead of three scenes a tick. Houseguest-days with no scene fell from about 9% (before ORW-323) to 17%; the rest are houseguests who spent the stretch alone or in the player's room.
- **Persistence of intent rose for the right reason**: 69% seek the same partner the next day, now driven by stored goals.
- **Balance concern 1: the folds are heavily ALLIANCE (73%)**, because the goal steps that serve "pull them in", "make a promise", "pitch" and "recruit" all fold as alliance; conflict fell from 23% to under 1% and betrayal to 0. A Big Brother house needs friction: chore fights only fire between people who already dislike each other, and `confront` is never chosen by any goal step. This will inflate trust and alignment over a season.
- **Balance concern 2:** `sceneMatchesInitiatorsDrive` dropped to 25%, but that metric reads the OLD drive layer; the new scenes follow the agenda instead, so it is no longer a fair measure (to be replaced by an agenda-match metric).
- Recommended follow-up (card): rebalance house-life folds and friction: give get-them-out / revenge steps a confront or campaign fold mix, let mood and low affinity raise friction, and fold recruiting as strategy until the alliance exists; then re-run this report. Measured and reported, never a gate (Ryne 2026-11-05/06).
